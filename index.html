About The Jamna

The Jamna Cafe and Restaurant is a family-owned establishment dedicated to serving the finest quality food and beverages. With over 15 years of experience, we pride ourselves on our warm hospitality, excellent service, and commitment to culinary excellence.

Our chef sources the freshest ingredients locally and internationally to create memorable dining experiences. Whether you're looking for a quick coffee, a casual lunch, or an elegant dinner, The Jamna is your perfect destination.
